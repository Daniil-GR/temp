How this site is made.

Article text was composed for Atlas and checked against public educational resources. The references below help readers verify ideas and learn more. Because the articles are introductory, they do not attempt to cover every nuance or recent research development.

All visual artwork is original SVG illustration made for this site, designed to convey geographical concepts. Images are schematic and must not be used as actual navigation charts, scale maps, or scientific datasets.

Independent publication

Atlas is not affiliated with NASA, NOAA, USGS, NPS, or any linked institution. Links are provided for educational context.

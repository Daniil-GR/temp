ABOUT ATLAS

Curiosity has
no borders.

An independent illustrated field guide for anyone who wants to understand a little more about the planet beneath their feet.

OUR PURPOSE

Make the familiar feel worth discovering.

Atlas brings together approachable explanations of geography, cartography, and Earth science. The goal is to help readers recognize the patterns behind landscapes, weathered rocks, water, and maps.

The project is intentionally small: a curated set of articles rather than an endless feed. Each story offers an entry point, not a claim to be an exhaustive scientific treatment.

Made for the curious.

The text is written for a general audience. Science is simplified when needed, with links to educational references so readers can investigate further.

Illustrated, not photographed.

All landscape artworks on this site are original interpretive SVG illustrations. They are not satellite images, measured maps, or authoritative geographic datasets.

EDITORIAL APPROACH

Clarity over spectacle.

We prioritize straightforward language and explanations supported by public resources from organizations such as USGS, NOAA, NASA, and the National Park Service.

Atlas is an independent informational site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by these agencies. Each article includes a small reading list.

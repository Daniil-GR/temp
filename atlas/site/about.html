About Atlas

Curiosity is a way
of seeing.

An independent educational guide to the planet — for anyone who has ever stopped to wonder why a landscape looks the way it does.

A closer look, not a louder voice.

Atlas brings together geography, cartography, and Earth science. We begin with familiar places and follow the less familiar processes behind them: the movement of ice, the work of rivers, the slow rise of mountains.

Our stories are introductions, not substitutes for specialist research. Each includes references so you can check the explanation and keep exploring.

Images with a place and a provenance.

Landscape photographs and historical maps are credited to their creators. Captions describe what is pictured; a photograph accompanying an explainer is not a claim of original field reporting. Our interactive globe uses a NASA satellite mosaic, not live imagery.

Built for a quieter web.

No accounts, adverts, tracking pixels, or subscription forms. Your reading list stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server when you save a story.
